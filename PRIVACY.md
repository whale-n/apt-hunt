# Privacy Policy — apt-hunt

apt-hunt is a personal, single-user tool that helps its owner search for an apartment. It is not offered to the public.

**Data accessed.** With the owner's consent, apt-hunt reads listing-alert emails in the owner's own Gmail account (only messages carrying the `apt-feed` label), adds labels to them, creates draft emails for the owner to review, and sends notification emails to the owner's own address. It never sends email to anyone else.

**Storage and sharing.** Extracted listing details are stored in the owner's own Notion workspace. Listing text and photos are sent to the Anthropic API for scoring. No data is sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

**Retention and deletion.** The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete stored data from their Notion workspace.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/whale-n/apt-hunt/issues.
